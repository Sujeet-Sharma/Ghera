Shipping Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. Custom orders may take longer.

Shipping Methods

We offer standard and express shipping options. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and chosen method.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are not included in the product price and will be calculated based on delivery location, weight, and courier partner.

Delivery Times

Standard shipping: 5-7 business days. Express shipping: 2-3 business days. International orders may take 10-14 business days.

Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email to monitor your package's progress.

Contact Us

For shipping inquiries, please contact us at support@gherabypriya.com.
