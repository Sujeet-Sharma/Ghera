Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: December 27, 2025

  • What is Ghera by Priya?
    Ghera by Priya is a premium Indian fashion label specializing in elegant, full-flare skirts crafted with refined craftsmanship and timeless design.
  • How can I place an order?
    Orders can be placed directly via WhatsApp or Instagram DM. Simply message us with the product name or image, and our team will assist you personally.
  • Do you have an online checkout?
    Our e-commerce website is currently in the launch phase. For now, all purchases are handled exclusively through WhatsApp and Instagram for a personalized experience.
  • What payment methods do you accept?
    We accept UPI, bank transfer, and other digital payment options shared during order confirmation.
  • Are delivery charges included in the product price?
    Delivery charges will be calculated based on your location.
  • How long does delivery take?
    Orders are typically dispatched within 3–5 business days. Delivery timelines may vary based on location.
  • Do you ship across India?
    Yes, we offer PAN-India shipping.
  • Are the skirts ready-to-wear?
    Yes. All our skirts are ready-to-wear with comfortable fits designed for effortless elegance.
  • Will the product look exactly the same as shown?
    We make every effort to display true colors. Minor variations may occur due to lighting or screen resolution.
  • What is your return policy?
    Returns are accepted only in case of manufacturing defects or incorrect items received, subject to our Return & Refund Policy.
  • Do you offer customization?
    Customization is currently not available. However, it may be introduced in future collections.
  • How can I contact customer support?
    You can reach us anytime via WhatsApp or Instagram DM for assistance.
