Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

At Ghera by Priya, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you interact with us.

Information We Collect

We collect limited personal information only when you contact us or place an order through WhatsApp or Instagram. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Contact number
  • Shipping address
  • Order details and preferences

We do not collect payment card or banking details directly.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Processing and confirming your orders
  • Communicating order updates
  • Providing customer support
  • Improving our products and services

We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your details are shared only when necessary to fulfill your order (such as with delivery partners).

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Communication happens through secure platforms like WhatsApp and Instagram; however, users are encouraged to share only necessary details.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@gherabypriya.com.
