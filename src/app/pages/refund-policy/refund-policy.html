Refund and Return Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

At Ghera by Priya, every piece is crafted with care and attention to detail. We strive to ensure complete satisfaction with every order. Please review our Return & Refund Policy carefully before placing an order.

Returns

We accept returns only in case of manufacturing defects or incorrect items received.

Any return request must be raised within 48 hours of delivery.

To initiate a return, please contact us via WhatsApp or Instagram DM with:

  • Order details
  • Clear photos/videos of the issue

Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original condition with tags intact.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Customized or altered products
  • Products damaged due to misuse, improper handling, or wear and tear
  • Minor color variations due to lighting or screen resolution

Refunds

Once the returned product is received and inspected, we will notify you about the approval status.

Approved refunds will be processed within 7–10 working days.

Refunds will be made via the original payment method or as mutually agreed.

Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges.

Customers may place a new order once the return is approved.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are non-refundable, unless the return is due to our error.

Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled within 12 hours of confirmation.

Once an order is shipped, cancellation requests will not be accepted.

Contact Us

For returns or exchanges, please email us at support@gherabypriya.com with your order number.
