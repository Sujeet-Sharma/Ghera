About Priya

With a deep appreciation for fabric, flow, and feminine silhouettes, Priya Sharma founded Ghera by Priya to bring thoughtfully designed skirts to modern Indian wardrobes. The brand begins its journey with a curated collection of skirts that blend comfort, versatility, and timeless style.

As the founder and designer, Priya is closely involved in every step—from selecting fabrics to refining fits and finishes. Balancing her professional career, entrepreneurship, and family life, she brings a practical yet creative perspective to her designs. Ghera by Priya is created for women who value effortless style, quality craftsmanship, and everyday elegance.
