Our Story

Ghera by Priya is a modern expression of refined simplicity. Founded by Priya Sharma, the brand begins with an exclusive skirt collection designed to flow effortlessly and feel timeless. Each piece reflects quiet luxury—where thoughtful design, premium fabrics, and flawless fit come together with ease.

Our Mission

Our mission is to craft elevated skirts that embody elegance, comfort, and enduring style. We design for women who appreciate subtle luxury, refined silhouettes, and uncompromising quality—pieces made to be worn, loved, and remembered.
